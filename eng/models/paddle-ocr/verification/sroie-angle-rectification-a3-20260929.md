# A3 known-angle quadrilateral rectification evidence (2026-09-29)

This follow-up reuses four real SROIE word crops from four receipt pages, transformed by known angles (`0`, `±12`, `±27`, `±90`, `180°`). Unlike the prior unrectified recognizer-only baseline, each run now supplies the exact transformed source quadrilateral to `OcrPipeline.RecognizeOnlyAsync`; therefore it evaluates geometric crop rectification, not automatic rotation-angle prediction.

## Results

For every one of the eight angles, both Perspective and `AffineWhenEquivalent` rectification recovered all four case-folded source strings exactly on ORT CPU and OpenVINO CPU:

- 32/32 variant × source-crop records per backend were exact under case-folded CER (`0%`).
- Perspective and affine text agreed on 32/32 records per backend.
- ORT and OpenVINO text agreed on 32/32 records for each transform; prepared tensor SHA also agreed across backends.
- The affine policy selected the affine path for 32/32 rotated rectangles.
- Source polygon round-trip max error was `0 px`.
- Perspective/affine prepared-tensor max absolute difference was `0` at `0°`, `±90°`, and `180°`; at `±12°`/`±27°` it was at most `0.0078432`, within the explicit interpolation tolerance `0.02`.

This contrasts with the prior unrectified recognizer-only run: at `±90°` all four crops were empty, and at `±12°`/`±27°` CER was high. Supplying the known quadrilateral rectifies the real crop correctly. It does **not** prove the detector or orientation classifier can find/predict that quadrilateral automatically, and the crops are still a small controlled rotation set rather than a natural rotation-distribution benchmark.

The full 64-record backend/transform JSON outputs are generated by `PaddleOcrSroieAngleIntegrationTests.KnownRotatedQuadRecoversTextWithPerspectiveAndSafeAffine` under test results. The source image/crop SHAs and model/dictionary SHAs are embedded in each row.
