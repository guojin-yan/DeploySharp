## Current development decision

Keep Clamp as the natural wide-line default and enhancement disabled by default. SlidingWindow is explicit opt-in: the corrected merger improves its result on the fixed HierText test cohort but still trails Clamp, and the later far-anchor rule used a train-split failure as development data. The FUNSD natural-scan enhancement evaluation also shows a net increase in character and word edits, so it does not justify a production default change. These are evidence-based limits, not missing full-matrix reruns. Further native or 5+50 runs are warranted only for a changed implementation, a new exact asset/backend combination or an independent quality sample that could change the support decision.
