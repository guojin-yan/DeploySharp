# HierText train natural wide-line seam holdout - PP-OCRv6 Small / Medium (2026-10-27)

The original A2 requirement concerns **normalized recognition width >3,200 pixels**, not 3,200 characters. This experiment uses original labelled quadrilaterals directly with `RecognizeOnlyAsync`, bypassing DET/CLS and detector-association misses. Source JPEGs remain outside the repository. This train-split selection was fixed without inspecting predictions and was not used to develop the preceding seam change. A later [far-anchor follow-up](paddleocr-hiertext-train-far-anchor-followup-20261027.md) used one observed failure to develop another seam change; this cohort is therefore **not independent validation for that later rule**. It is also not proven independent of the recognizers' original training data.

Explicit invariant-lowercase results are recorded separately in JSON; they need not equal the raw metrics. Spaces and punctuation are not removed. These corpus/micro rates differ from historical detector-association macro means and should not be compared as if they used the same input or protocol.

All **48/48** calls pass width/source/CTC contracts: original source region and coordinates survive, each raw window is <=3,200 pixels, coverage is ordered and overlapping from 0 to 1, and retained emitted tokens reconstruct the final text.
