# PP-OCRv6 natural wide-line far-anchor seam follow-up (2026-10-27)

This is a merge-only development follow-up to the [prediction-blind train selection](paddleocr-hiertext-train-natural-wide-holdout-20261027.md). The selection preceded inference, but one observed failure was then used to design this change. **The train cohort is no longer independent validation for the far-anchor rule.** The separate HierText test cohort below is reused only as a regression check, not as fresh blind validation.

The previous bounded seam matcher searched up to three left-window CTC steps before the geometric overlap. On `f96269dcce54ad5c/12/0`, the matching right prefix starts farther left because approximate CTC token positions drift; Small and Medium each kept about 35 duplicate characters. The new fallback searches at most ten CTC steps or 20% of the overlap, whichever is smaller. It requires exactly one identical three-token prefix anchor across the eligible left suffix and still applies the existing full-overlap geometry, edit-budget and ambiguity checks. If the anchor is repeated or alignment fails, the right prefix is retained and the seam remains uncertain. Clamp and raw recognizer output are unchanged.
